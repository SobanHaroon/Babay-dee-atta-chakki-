## Distance, fees and tokens

Geoapify Routing uses `drive`, metric units and latitude,longitude waypoints; GeoJSON coordinates are parsed as longitude,latitude. Metres are converted once. Failed routing returns an error, never a straight-line fee. Server service-region and road-distance checks apply after pin confirmation. These conventions follow the [Geoapify routing documentation](https://apidocs.geoapify.com/docs/routing/).

The existing observed policy remains Rs. 50/km, Rs. 50 minimum, nearest whole rupee and 45 km maximum; actual hosting/database values must be verified before release. No pricing change was approved or introduced. Ten-minute HMAC quotes bind the original address hash, address version, checkout context, destination, store, fee and pricing configuration/version. Candidate tokens preserve trusted match metadata through pin adjustment. Production secrets must be stable across server instances and separate from provider keys.

Only non-sensitive area candidate searches are cached, in a bounded five-minute process cache. Customer quotes/routes and prices are not shared through that cache. Lookup attempts have five-second provider timeouts and a 25-second resolver budget. Auth/quota failures stop immediately; a slow full query can fall back within the budget. Stage logs contain neither full addresses nor keys.

Supply server-only `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` or `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, rotated Geoapify geocoding/routing credentials, and independent random `DELIVERY_QUOTE_SECRET`, `ORDER_ACCESS_SECRET`, `RATE_LIMIT_SECRET` values of at least 32 characters. Verify `STORE_LATITUDE`, `STORE_LONGITUDE`, rate/minimum/limit/rounding configuration, `DELIVERY_PRICING_VERSION` and owner-approved `DELIVERY_SERVICE_BOUNDS`. Set exact `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` and appropriate proxy settings. Keep `APP_ENV=staging` and `ENABLE_CUSTOMER_NOTIFICATIONS=false` during verification. Owner-confirmed sale units, stock and pack weights are required before products become purchasable. Record rice grades without inventing them. Supply approved privacy/delivery/returns URLs for the accessible policy links; no policies were invented. Admin removal means inventory management, status updates and review moderation require trusted operational tools outside this storefront.

Legacy orders have not been migrated to opaque access tokens. Plan a secure owner-assisted recovery/migration procedure before release; do not restore phone-only public access. Preserve signing keys for existing order access and plan any future rotation explicitly.
